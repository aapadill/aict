# Governance Note: Recruiting Assistant Pilot

## Current Review Process

The HR team currently reviews candidates manually in the applicant tracking system. For the pilot, recruiters will see model-generated summaries and rankings before deciding whether to invite a candidate to interview.

## Planned Controls

The team plans to require recruiter review before any candidate is rejected or advanced. Recruiters will be instructed that model scores are advisory and must not be treated as the sole basis for interview or hiring decisions.

## Missing Bias And Validation Evidence

Bias testing has not been completed for the pilot population. The team has not yet reviewed subgroup performance, false negative rates, accessibility impacts, or whether historical hiring data reflects past discriminatory patterns.

## Monitoring Gaps

The team has not approved a post-deployment monitoring plan. Open questions include who reviews drift, how often performance is checked, what incident thresholds trigger escalation, and how recruiter overrides are audited.

## Documentation Gaps

The DPIA is planned but not complete. The team still needs a formal intended-purpose statement, provider and deployer role analysis, user instructions, candidate notice language, logging policy, retention policy, and escalation process.

## Pilot Decision

The governance team does not consider the assistant ready for unrestricted rollout. A limited pilot may be considered after the missing documentation, bias testing, monitoring plan, and human oversight procedure are completed.
