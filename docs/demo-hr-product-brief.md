# Product Brief: EU Recruiting Assistant

## Overview

The HR team plans to pilot an AI recruiting assistant for EU hiring processes. The tool screens job applications, parses CVs, summarizes candidate profiles, scores applicants, and produces a ranked shortlist for recruiter review.

## Intended Use

The intended use is decision support for recruiters during early-stage hiring. The system is not intended to make a final hiring decision automatically. Recruiters remain responsible for deciding which candidates move to interview.

## Users

Primary users are internal recruiters, hiring managers, and HR operations staff. The affected persons are job applicants and candidates whose application materials are processed and ranked.

## Input Data

Input data includes CVs, cover letters, application form answers, work history, education history, skills, certifications, job preferences, recruiter notes, and the job description for the open role.

## Outputs

The assistant outputs candidate summaries, skill-match notes, suitability scores, ranked shortlists, and recommended next actions such as "review", "interview", or "do not prioritize". The outputs are shown inside the existing applicant tracking workflow.

## Deployment Context

The pilot is planned for EU hiring teams. The tool will be used before interview selection and may influence which candidates receive recruiter attention. The deployment context is employment and worker-management.

## Automation And Oversight

The tool automatically generates summaries, scores, and rankings, but recruiters review the recommendations before interview invitations are sent. The product brief does not yet define a formal override procedure, appeal route, or monitoring threshold.
