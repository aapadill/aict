# Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 - selected demo reference excerpts

## Article 3(1) - AI system

"AI system" means a machine-based system that is designed to operate with varying levels of autonomy and that may exhibit adaptiveness after deployment, and that, for explicit or implicit objectives, infers, from the input it receives, how to generate outputs such as predictions, content, recommendations, or decisions that can influence physical or virtual environments.

## Article 5 - Prohibited AI practices

Article 5 includes prohibitions concerning AI systems that deploy subliminal, purposefully manipulative or deceptive techniques causing or likely causing significant harm; exploit vulnerabilities due to age, disability or a specific social or economic situation causing or likely causing significant harm; perform social scoring leading to detrimental or unfavourable treatment; create or expand facial recognition databases through untargeted scraping; infer emotions in workplace or education institutions except for medical or safety reasons; and perform certain biometric categorisation, predictive policing, or real-time remote biometric identification practices subject to the Regulation's conditions and exceptions.

## Annex III - High-risk areas

Annex III lists high-risk areas including biometrics, critical infrastructure, education and vocational training, employment and workers management and access to self-employment, access to and enjoyment of essential private services and essential public services and benefits, law enforcement, migration, asylum and border control management, and administration of justice and democratic processes.

## Article 50 - Transparency obligations

Article 50 includes transparency obligations for providers and deployers of certain AI systems, including systems intended to interact directly with natural persons, systems generating synthetic audio, image, video or text content, emotion recognition systems, biometric categorisation systems, and AI systems used to generate or manipulate deep fake content.

## Article 53 - Obligations for providers of general-purpose AI models

Article 53 includes obligations for providers of general-purpose AI models, including drawing up and keeping technical documentation, making information and documentation available to downstream providers that intend to integrate the model into AI systems, putting in place a policy to comply with Union copyright law, and drawing up and making publicly available a sufficiently detailed summary about content used for model training, according to the template provided by the AI Office.
